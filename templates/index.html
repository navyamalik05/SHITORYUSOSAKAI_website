FOUNDER

Sōke Kenwa Mabuni

Founder of Shitoryū Karate

Kenwa Mabuni (1889-1952) was a legendary Okinawan master who founded Shitoryū (Shitoryu) Karate. He dedicated his life to preserving traditional Okinawan karate and blending it with techniques from other styles to create a comprehensive martial system.

His philosophy emphasized:

  • Unity of martial arts (合一 - Gō Ichi)
  • Respect for tradition and innovation
  • Development of moral character
  • Service to society through martial arts
